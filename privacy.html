Little Soles

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Little Soles is a marketplace where parents buy and sell kids' shoes from each other. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it. Plain language, no fine print games.

The short version

  • We collect the basics needed to run your account and your orders.
  • Parents enter their kids' shoe sizes so we can match listings. That is the only child-related info we hold, and a parent enters it.
  • We do not run ads. We do not sell your data. We do not load advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Payments run through Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • You can delete your account and your data anytime.

Who runs Little Soles

Little Soles is operated by Firmware Foundation Studios. If you have a privacy question, contact us at the email in the Contact section below.

What we collect

Account info. Your name and email when you sign up. A password, stored only as a secure hash, if you sign up with email. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google instead, we store the account identifier those services give us, not a password.

Kids' sizes, entered by you. The shoe sizes and a first name or nickname you add for your kids, so the app can show the right listings and send size-match alerts. This is optional. The app works without it, and you can remove it anytime. We do not ask children to use the app or enter their own information.

Listings and orders. What you list, buy, sell, offer, and favorite, plus shipping details needed to complete an order.

Basic technical info. Standard app and device information needed to keep the app running and secure, such as error logs. Used to operate and fix the app, not to profile you.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising in the app.
  • No ad networks, no third-party trackers, no cross-app analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you pay or get paid, Stripe handles your card and bank details directly. We do not store your full card number. Stripe's handling of that data is covered by Stripe's own privacy policy.

Selling on Little Soles is free. Buyers pay an LS Protect fee on each order (7% of the item price plus $0.70), shown as its own line at checkout. That fee funds the LS Protect promise: a refund or credit if an item is not as described.

How we use your info

  • To run your account and log you in.
  • To show listings, match sizes, and send size-match alerts you asked for.
  • To process orders, payments, and LS Protect claims.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account or an order.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to run the service:

  • Stripe, to process payments.
  • The other parent in your transaction, who gets the info needed to complete the deal, such as a shipping address.
  • Law enforcement or legal authorities, only if the law requires it.

We do not share your data for advertising. We do not sell it.

Data retention

We keep your account info while your account is open. When you delete your account, we remove your personal data, except where we are legally required to keep a record, such as payment and tax records tied to a completed sale.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and your personal data at any time. In the app, open your Profile, then choose Delete Account. Or email us at the Contact address below and ask us to delete your account.

When you delete, we remove your profile, your kids' sizes, your favorites, and your listings. Records tied to completed payments may be retained where the law requires.

Children's privacy

Little Soles is for parents and guardians, not for children. We do not knowingly let children create accounts or collect information directly from children. The only child-related data we hold is the shoe size and nickname a parent chooses to enter. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Update or remove your kids' sizes anytime in the app.
  • Turn size-match alerts on or off.
  • Delete your account anytime.
  • Email us with any privacy request and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a real way, we will let you know in the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions about your privacy or a data request? Reach us at support@littlesoles.com or through the Help and Resources section inside the app.
